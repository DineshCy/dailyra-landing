Delete your account

Last updated: November 2026

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@dailyra.co.in with the subject line “Delete My Account Request”. Please write from the email address you use to sign in to Dailyra so we can verify the account.

What gets deleted

When your request is processed, we permanently delete your account and all data linked to it: your email address, profile, financial transactions, fitness and health logs, diet and food entries, notes, and step counts.

Timeline

We delete your data within 7 business days of verifying your request and confirm by email. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data we may keep

Payment records for Pro purchases are held by Razorpay under its own policies and legal obligations. Dailyra does not keep your card or bank details. Notification data from Auto-Detect Transactions is never uploaded, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Already signed in?

If you are signed in to the app you can also open the in-app delete account page. Questions? See our Privacy Policy.
