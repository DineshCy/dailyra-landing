UPI Auto-Detect, you stay in control

Turn it on and Dailyra suggests expenses from your bank and UPI payment notifications. Nothing is added until you approve it.

  • Uses notification access that you grant on your phone. It does not read your SMS inbox.

Questions

Good to know

Is Dailyra free?

Yes. You can start free with core features. Pro unlocks unlimited entries and the AI features.

Does Dailyra read my SMS?

No. The optional UPI Auto-Detect uses notification access that you grant yourself. OTPs and personal messages are ignored and never stored or uploaded, and every suggested expense needs your approval.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored in a secured cloud database where only your account can access your own records. We never sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the Delete account page. We delete your data within 7 business days.

Which phones does it work on?

Dailyra is built for Android phones and distributed on Google Play. Check the Google Play page for compatibility with your device.
