Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

📋 What data we collect

Dailyra collects and stores the following data to provide its services: user email address for authentication, financial transaction data you enter, fitness and health data you log, diet and food entries you create, personal notes you write, and profile information you provide.

🔒 How we store your data

All data is stored securely in Supabase cloud database with Row Level Security — meaning only you can access your own data. We never sell your personal data to third parties. We never share your data with advertisers. Data is used only to provide Dailyra services to you.

📱 Ads & third-party services

We use Google AdMob to show ads to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalisation. You can opt out by upgrading to Dailyra Pro.

🗑️ Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@dailyra.co.in. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
