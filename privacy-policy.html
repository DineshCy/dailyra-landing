Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What data we collect

Dailyra collects and stores the following data to provide its services: user email address for authentication, financial transaction data you enter, fitness and health data you log, diet and food entries you create, personal notes you write, and profile information you provide.

How we store your data

All data is stored securely in Supabase cloud database with Row Level Security, meaning only you can access your own data. We never sell your personal data to third parties. We never share your data with advertisers. Data is used only to provide Dailyra services to you.

Visitor analytics

Lovable visitor analytics is enabled on the Dailyra web app. It records pages visited, where visitors came from, their country, browser, operating system, device type, how long they stay, page load speed, errors, and clicks on buttons and links. It uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device, does not keep IP addresses, and does not identify visitors. Lovable collects this data for us. Lovable's privacy policy explains how it handles it. Detailed data is kept for 90 days and daily summaries are kept for a year, including visit and page-view counts and the pages seen and referral source for each visit.

Notification access (optional)

If you enable Auto-Detect Transactions, Dailyra reads payment and bank notifications on your device only to detect transaction amounts. OTPs, personal chats and other notifications are ignored and never stored or uploaded. Parsed data is saved only after you approve it. You can turn this off at any time from your phone settings.

Third-party services

Dailyra uses Supabase for account sign-in and database storage. Google receives the information needed to authenticate your account if you choose Google sign-in. For Pro purchases, Dailyra sends your name and email to Razorpay for checkout, and Razorpay processes the payment details you provide.

Camera, photos and step counting (optional)

If you use Food Analyser, the photo you take or choose is sent securely to our AI service only to estimate nutrition, and is not shared for advertising. Step counting (optional) uses your device's activity sensor and the counts are saved only in your account.

Age requirement

Dailyra is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Follow the steps on our Delete account page, or email support@dailyra.co.in with the subject line “Delete My Account Request”. We permanently delete your data within 7 business days and confirm by email.
